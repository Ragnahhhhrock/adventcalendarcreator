Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. Making and opening your digital calendar is free, with no account needed.

Where are my photos stored?

On your own device, inside your browser. They aren't uploaded anywhere, so use the same phone or browser each day. Clearing site data will remove them.

Can I start before December?

Yes. Build it any time. Doors unlock on their date in December, and a preview-date setting lets you test it early.

Can I send it to someone?

Not yet. Calendars currently live on the device that made them. Sharing by link is on the list.

Does it work on a phone?

It's built phone-first. Add photos from your camera roll or take new ones, then pinch and drag to decorate.
